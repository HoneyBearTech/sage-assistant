Sage

Welcome to Sage. A few steps get it ready; you can change everything later in Settings.

Where should Sage think?

On this computer, nothing you type leaves the PC. Online, Sage can use Claude, OpenAI, Gemini or another service with your own key: what you type goes to that company, which charges per use (Gemini has a free tier for some models). Sage can't cap what you spend, so set a limit on the company's own website. You choose one, and save its key, in Settings.

The local model runner

Sage thinks on this computer through a separate program, the local model runner.

  1. Open the install guide and follow its steps to install the local model runner.
  2. Start the runner.
  3. Come back here and choose Check again.

Choose a model

The model is what Sage thinks with. The local model runner downloads it once. Choosing a model here makes Sage think on this computer, even if it uses an online service now.

            Voice

            Hold Ctrl+Space, or hold the Talk button, while you speak. Your words go into the message box and are sent only when you press Enter. Speech is worked out on this PC.

            Home

            The first time Sage opens on a new day, Home shows the daily brief: today's reminders and your latest notes. It is read aloud only when Read replies aloud is set to Always. The brief is made on this PC and never sent to the brain.

            Look

            Sound effects are switched on and off with Sound in the top bar.
